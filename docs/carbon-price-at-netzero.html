Carbon Price at Net Zero

Of 203 scenarios flagged as reaching net zero, 23 also report a model-derived carbon price at the end of the pathway — assumed tax rates and statutory ETS prices excluded. In real 2025 dollars they run from $63 to $4,189 per tonne CO₂e.

  • The spread is the finding. A ~67× range between the cheapest and most expensive net-zero shadow price, driven by how much the model leans on carbon dioxide removal and how late neutrality arrives.
  • Removals cut the price. The one paper with a paired comparison (p562) needs $554/tCO₂ with CDR and $2,768 without — a 5× jump. The global run (Global) sits at $4,189 by 2050.
  • The low end is regional. The cheapest entries (p282, Global, $63–63) come from one study; the rest cluster from about $300 upward.
  • One Global study (p437) contributes roughly a quarter of the 23 (26%), all in the upper half ($398–4,189); it weights the distribution toward high values.

  • Net-zero membership comes from netzero-flags.csv (is_netzero == True) — the same filter as the sector-balance figure, but here no sector breakdown is required. Scenario and area are matched on their canonical keys, so "NZE" and "2050 NZE" join.
  • Shadow-price rows are harmonized-costs.csv rows with Cost_Type = "Carbon price or abatement cost" and a USD2025/… value. Rows whose Cost_Type_Detail names a policy level — CO2 tax, quota price, … price assumption, constant carbon tax, assumed … tax — are dropped (39 rows); “tax required to achieve” phrasings are kept as derived values. A price whose scenario field is a catch-all ("All scenarios", "Model assumption", blank) is applied to every flagged scenario of that paper that has no price of its own (1 rows here).
  • Unit normalisation to 2025 USD per tCO₂e: /tCO2, /tCO2eq, /tonCO2, /Mg CO2eq, /t → ×1; /kgCO2eq → ×1000; /ktCO2 → ×0.001; /tC → ×44/12; /yr and unrecognised → dropped. CO₂ and CO₂e price bases are pooled.
  • End year = the scenario’s neutrality year from netzero-flags.csv. The price is read at that year, or the nearest earlier year the scenario reports one (price_year in the CSV). One value per scenario — the median if several rows share that year.

Caveats

  • n = 23, and one study (p437) is 6 of them — 26% of the sample, all high.
  • CO₂ and CO₂e price bases are pooled — a modest distortion for carbon prices, larger for GHG-basket abatement costs.
  • The policy / shadow split is drawn from Cost_Type_Detail keywords; a handful of ambiguous phrasings (“national carbon market price”, “reference CO₂ price”) are kept and could be model assumptions rather than outputs.
  • Net-zero years span 2040–2070; later dates tend to carry higher end-of-pathway prices, and the sample is too thin to separate that from study-to-study variation.
  • The net-zero flag has a threshold (--netzero-fraction, default 0.05); a looser value adds scenarios, but the carbon-price bottleneck is that most net-zero scenarios don't report one.
