CAPEX by Technology

The reviewed net-zero studies state an overnight capital cost for the power technologies they deploy. Pooled across 14 papers, 230 values for 15 technologies — and the studies disagree with each other by more than any single technology's cost falls over three decades.

What it shows

14papers
43.0×widest disagreement (Hydrogen turbine/FC)
  • The ordering is conventional. Solar PV and onshore wind sit cheapest (median around $1.17M/MW), nuclear, geothermal and BECCS-type bioenergy most expensive (up to $8.66M/MW for Concentrated solar).
  • The within-technology spread is the story. Even for a mature technology the studies span a 43.0× range (Hydrogen turbine/FC) — base year, region, financing assumptions and what the "plant" includes all differ and are not always recorded.
  • Learning is priced in. Where a study gives both a near-term and a long-term number, the assumed cost falls — Bioenergy drops from $10.83M to $2.44M/MW (77% lower) between the two horizons.
  • By world region (median across all technologies, so composition-weighted): Rest of Asia-Pacific $1.47M (n=17) · China $1.50M (n=37) · Middle East $1.83M (n=26) · Europe $2.42M (n=74) · Global / multi-region $3.16M (n=22) · Africa $7.94M (n=25) · Not reported $7.94M (n=29). Regions with only one or two contributing studies should not be read as a regional cost signal.

Method

→
2 de-duplicate
→
3 classify technology
→
4 tag region + horizon
  • Not net-zero filtered. A CAPEX number is a techno-economic input, usually stated once for the whole study, so every USD2025/MW row of harmonized-costs.csv is used (365 rows before cleaning).
  • Cleaning. Fixed-O&M rows (USD2025/MW.yr) are a different unit and never enter here; non-generation equipment (boilers, heat pumps, lighting, motors) is dropped by keyword (27 rows). Exact duplicates on (paper, label, year, value) are collapsed.
  • Technology is matched from Cost_Type_Detail / Sector_Other against a fixed pattern list — CCS and offshore variants are tested before the generic "gas" / "wind" patterns. 2 rows matched no pattern and are excluded.
  • Time horizon (marker shape) from the row's Year: near-term ≤ 2030, long-term ≥ 2035, otherwise unspecified.
  • World region (marker colour) is keyword-matched from the Area string; a country or sub-national unit maps to its region, an unrecognised or blank area is Not reported.
  • Cut. A technology needs ≥ 5 values from ≥ 2 papers to get its own row. Dropped for thinness: Battery storage (6r/1p), Electrolyser (2r/1p), CHP (2r/1p), Wave / tidal (1r/1p), Municipal-waste power (1r/1p).

Caveats

  • These are assumptions, not outcomes — what each model was told a technology costs, not a result of the scenario.
  • Currency base year is harmonised to 2025 USD, but the physical scope of "$/MW" is not: some values are bare equipment, others include grid connection, construction financing or storage.
  • Region is pooled. A gas turbine in Saudi Arabia and one in Portugal are on the same row.
  • Near-term / long-term is the row's stated cost year, which many studies leave blank — the unspecified points carry no learning information.
  • Bioenergy mixes plain biomass combustion with BECCS full-chain costs; its high tail is partly that.
