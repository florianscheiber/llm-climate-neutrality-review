Unit Harmonization Method

The extracted climate-scenario tables report quantities in 453 distinct unit strings — GW, PJ biomass year-1, 1975$/GJ, Tg CO2eq a-1. This step collapses them onto five target units, converting currencies through historical exchange rates and indexing every monetary value to real 2025 US dollars.

52.0  1975 $/GJ
 → × 3.6           187.2  USD/MWh  (GJ → MWh)
 → × 1.00          187.2  USD/MWh  (USD, no FX)
 → × 1.20          224.64  USD2025/MWh (US deflator 1975→2025)
Every harmonized number carries the exchange rate, deflator factor and physical factor that produced it, plus a status flag.

Scope

Harmonization applies to every value / unit pair in the three merged tables: Capacity, Amount, Investment_Cost and Variable_Cost in mitigation; Value in costs and in emissions. It also harmonizes the Sector field (below). Standardized values and their provenance are added as new columns; an original value is never overwritten, though a blank one may be completed from sibling rows (see Context fill, logged in Context_Filled).

Everything outside these five dimensions — percentages and shares, index numbers, physical intensities (W/m², kWh/capita), counts (wind turbines), non-CO₂ gases (ktCH4, Mt NOx), water volumes — passes through unchanged with a status flag. It is not forced into a target unit.

Context fill

Before any unit is touched, blanks that the surrounding rows resolve unambiguously are filled in. The extractor frequently records a field on only some rows of a paper — most often Area: a single-country study that labels only a handful of its rows and leaves the rest empty (the Chile paper p436 carried Chile on 19 rows and blank on 126).

For each paper, a field is filled from its other rows only when they hold exactly one distinct non-blank value for it — i.e. the paper is internally unambiguous. A paper spanning several regions (USA, China, Global…) leaves its blank Area rows untouched. Nothing is inferred across papers, and a non-blank value is never overwritten.

Every fill is listed per row in the new Context_Filled column (";"-joined field names, blank when nothing was filled), so each one can be audited or reverted. In the current corpus this fills 574 blank Area values (126 in emissions — all the Chile paper — and 448 in mitigation) and 561 mitigation cost base-years; the costs table needs none.

Canonical keys

The same scenario or area is written many ways across a paper ("NZ_2050" / "NZ 2050" / "NZ-2050"; "USA" / "United States" / "U.S."). Two columns, Scenario_canon and Area_canon, hold a normalised form — unified case and separators, net-zero spelling variants merged, well-known country aliases folded. Distinguishing tokens such as target years are kept, so NZ 2040 and NZ 2050 stay separate. Every downstream group-by and every netzero-flags.csv join runs on these keys, not the raw strings, so a scenario is no longer split into sub-threshold fragments.

01   Normalize the unit string

The raw strings are irregular: 177 distinct forms in the mitigation Amount column alone, 160 in costs, 116 in emissions. A regex pass fixes the recurring patterns observed across the corpus:

  • Unicode → ASCII: CO₂, ⋅, ⁻¹, superscripts, en-dashes.
  • Spelling variants collapsed: CO2-eq / CO2e / CO2 (equiv.) → CO2eq; tonnes / metric tons → t; kilotons → kt; standard coal equivalent → tce.
  • Inverse-power time notation: yr-1, a−1, p.a. → /yr.
  • Magnitude words and scientific notation pulled out as a numeric multiplier: billion, trillion, Mrd, bn, 10^6, ×10^4, a bare leading 1000 JPY/kW, and currency-glued prefixes M€, T$, bAUD.
  • An embedded price year is extracted for the monetary step: 1975$/GJ, €2016/MWh, bAUD2021.
  • Descriptor words dropped before classification: PJ biomass year-1, GWh electricity.

The string is then split into a numerator and one or more denominators.

02   Classify the dimension

The numerator is matched against curated token tables — power, energy, CO₂/carbon, area, currency. A bare mass token (Mt, 10^6 t) is read as CO₂ when the row is an emissions row, or a mitigation row whose measure is inherently a CO₂ quantity (carbon dioxide removal, CCS, CCU); otherwise it stays unclassified, because it could be ammonia, hydrogen or materials. Anything matching a percentage / index / count / intensity pattern is marked non-convertible.

Generic assumptions

Non-regional, applied uniformly, and flagged in _status:

  • 1 toe = 11.63 MWh  ·  1 tce = 8.141 MWh  ·  1 bcm gas = 10.6 TWh
  • elemental carbon → CO₂ × 44/12, for tC, MtC, TgC, “carbon”
  • CO2eq is kept as a separate target unit from CO2; no GWP horizon is imposed

Sector harmonization

The extraction schema's Sector is noisy: Land-Use and Forestry are split, and Sector = “Other” holds ~540 rows that are really transport, buildings or livestock. The free-text Sector_Other carries the real signal — 315 distinct strings across the three tables. Three columns are added:

ColumnHow
Sector_stdLand-Use + Forestry → LULUCF; then, when Sector is Other/blank, reclassified from a Sector_Other keyword rule (transport / buildings / services → Energy, cement / steel / chemicals → Industry, livestock / crops → Agriculture, AFOLU / forest → LULUCF, economy-wide → All sectors)
Subsector_stdcanonical subsector from Sector_Other — ~30 buckets: Power: generation / storage / T&D, Heat, Transport, Buildings, Hydrogen & synthetic fuels, Bioenergy, Fossil fuel supply, Industry: cement / iron & steel / chemicals / pulp & paper / food / other, Agriculture: livestock / crops, CDR: DACCS / BECCS / land, Waste, Services, Whole economy …
Sector_std_sourcelanduse_merge / from_sector_other / original

Coverage: 98% of the 3,616 rows with a Sector_Other get a Subsector_std. The Other main-sector bucket shrinks from ~540 rows to ~35 (genuinely mixed — “energy, industrial and urban”, “non-energy sources”). DACCS is filed under Energy with the subsector flagging it.

Monetary conversion

Convert first, then deflate with the US GDP deflator — the convention used by IEA, NREL ATB and IPCC WG3, which this dataset will be compared against.

nominal LCU (year Y)
 → ÷ FX(Y)    nominal USD (year Y)  World Bank annual-average rate, LCU per USD
 → × D₃₀₂₅/Dᵧ real USD 2025  US GDP deflator ratio

Base year Y

Resolved in order, and the source recorded in _base_year_src:

  • explicit — the Currency_Base_Year the model extracted (present for ~40% of cost rows)
  • unit_string — a year embedded in the unit (2010 US$/kW)
  • publication_year — fallback to the paper’s year; flagged, since it assumes the cost is quoted in then-current money

netzero-flags.csv

A companion table, one row per (paper, scenario canon, area canon, basis) that carries net-zero evidence. Scenario and area are the canonical keys (see Context fill / the shared key module), so "NZ_2050", "NZ 2050" and "NZ-2050" are one scenario. f is --netzero-fraction (default 0.05) and is written into every row. series_source records which tier decided the flag, most reliable first:

series_sourceEvidenceFlagged net zero when
all_sectors_total≥ 2 years of an economy-wide All sectors totallast ≤ f × first
sector_sum≥ 2 years of a ≥ 2-sector sumlast ≤ f × first
single_endpoint_totalone late All sectors reading (year ≥ 2035), no first yearemissions ≤ 0
single_endpoint_sector_sumone late ≥ 2-sector sumsum ≤ 0
relative_reductiona late −95 % vs base / % of YYYY / Index (YYYY=100) pointremaining fraction ≤ f

Net-negative end states always qualify. ratio_last_to_first (or, for a relative row, the remaining fraction) is written so the threshold can be moved without re-running. A percentage / index row only counts when its Emission_Type is a CO₂ series and its wording is a level or a reduction, not a rate or a share.

Status vocabulary

  • ok converted with no assumptions
  • base_year=from_publication  assumed_currency  assumed_co2  carbon_to_co2  generic_calorific  fx_year=YYYY — converted, one documented assumption
  • denom_kept:… numerator converted, an unrecognized denominator left verbatim
  • not_convertible percentage, index, ratio — no target unit exists
  • unit_unparsed  unit_not_reported  no_value — nothing to convert

Known limitations

  • Bare mass in a non-CO₂ mitigation row (Mt, 10^6 t) is left unparsed rather than guessed — it may be ammonia, hydrogen, or materials.
  • Other GHGs (ktCH4, Mt NOx, Mt BC, Mt CO) are excluded — the target is CO₂ mass only.
  • $ without a country marker is assumed USD; a handful of these may be AUD or CAD.
  • Value ranges (300–400) are read as their lower bound; the extraction prompt now emits lower / upper / midpoint as separate rows, so these are rare.
  • The euro-area aggregate deflator and rate are used for all EUR values regardless of the study’s country.
