Electricity Price at Net Zero

Of 203 scenarios flagged as reaching net zero, 13 also report a system electricity price at the end of the pathway. In real 2025 dollars they run from $62 to $268 per MWh — a 4.3× spread across 5 studies.

  • A 4.3× spread. The cheapest net-zero electricity price (p462, Saudi Arabia, $62/MWh) and the most expensive (p137, WA, $268) differ by more than the whole range of today's wholesale markets — this is a modelling assumption gap, not a physical one.
  • Removals barely move the price. The one paper with a paired comparison (p562) lands at $126/MWh with CDR and $141 without — a $15/MWh difference.
  • Median by world region: Middle East $64 (n=4) · China $120 (n=7) · Europe $134 (n=2). Each region is one or two studies, so region and study effect are not separable here — within a study the scenarios sit close together, the gap is between studies / regions.
  • One study (p462, Saudi Arabia) contributes 4 of the 13 scenarios (31%), all at the high end ($62–66); it pulls the median up.

Method

1 net-zero filter
→
2 electricity-price rows
→
3 drop single-tech tariffs
→
4 price at the neutrality year
  • Net-zero membership comes from netzero-flags.csv (is_netzero == True) — the same filter as the carbon-price and sector-balance figures, matched on canonical scenario / area keys.
  • Electricity-price rows are harmonized-costs.csv rows with Cost_Type = "Electricity price" and a value already in USD2025/MWh. Rows whose Cost_Type_Detail names a single-technology on-grid tariff, a feed-in tariff, a PPA or an LCOE quote — and a mislabelled staple-food series in one paper — are dropped (123 rows). A price whose scenario field is a catch-all is applied to every flagged scenario of that paper that has none of its own (2 rows here).
  • End year = the scenario’s neutrality year from netzero-flags.csv; the price is read at that year or the nearest earlier one the scenario reports (price_year in the CSV). One value per (paper, scenario) — the median across every area and row at that year, so a study that quotes the same price for four grid regions counts once.
  • World region is keyword-matched from the Area string (country / sub-national unit → region); a scenario spanning regions is filed as Global / multi-region.

Caveats

  • n = 13 from only 5 papers, and one study (p462) is 31% of it. Treat this as a range of modelling assumptions, not a distribution.
  • “System electricity price” is not defined identically across studies — wholesale, retail, LCOE-of-the-system and shadow price of the electricity balance are pooled here.
  • Retail vs wholesale is the likely driver of the high cluster; the extraction does not always record which.
  • Net-zero years span 2036–2060; a later date is not obviously cheaper or dearer in this sample.
  • The net-zero flag has a threshold (--netzero-fraction, default 0.05); the real bottleneck is that most net-zero scenarios report no electricity price at all.
